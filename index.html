HOW IT FIGHTS BACK

HEAVIER SCROLL

The longer you scroll, the heavier it gets. On supported feeds, pause cards add stopping points as you scroll.

NO SLOT LEVER

Pull-to-refresh goes quiet and reloads have a cooldown. No more random rewards on demand.

AUTOPLAY OFF

Hide supported short-video feeds and pause autoplay. Coverage can change when sites update.

SHIELDS + BREATHING

A shield offers a notification to open Curbies, or you can open the app yourself. Complete the pause and intention prompt to request a limited access window.

THE PACT

Keep rules editable or choose a pact. Loosening then waits your selected cooling-off period and requires you to return and confirm.

TRAINER SCHOOL

Ten short lessons on common engagement patterns, with source links and practical ways to notice them.

WIN BY STOPPING (PRO+)

Optional mini-games or an equivalent quiet pause. Collect stickers for choosing to stop; holographic milestones count cumulative protected days.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Screen Time selections and browsing activity stay on your device. Optional Sign in with Apple enables progress sync through your own iCloud. Purchases are handled by the App Store. Read the privacy policy.

PLANNED PRICING

These are proposed USD prices. The App Store will confirm the local price, billing period, trial eligibility and renewal terms before any purchase.

LITE · FREE

Native app shields, opening limits, a deliberate gate, basic lessons and the Curbie Dex. Protection can stay on with editable rules.

PRO · $19.99/YEAR

Everything in Lite plus full Safari scroll controls, stopping points, live training and all lessons. Or $2.99/month. Trial availability and pricing are shown by the App Store. Optional reminders use notifications on your phone.

PRO+ · $29.99/YEAR

Everything in Pro plus the arcade (six mini-games), collectable and holographic stickers, and the Glass and Pop Art looks. Trial availability is shown by the App Store.
