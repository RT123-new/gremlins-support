HOW IT WORKS

  1. 1

    PICK YOUR RIVALS

    Choose the apps that keep winning. Each one gets a gate. Screen Time keeps your choices on your phone; Curbies only gets anonymous tokens.

  2. 2

    MEET THE GATE

    Open one and a curbie blocks the way. Breathe for a few seconds, say what you're there for, pick how long. Or walk away.

  3. 3

    SLOW THE WEB VERSION

    Still want Instagram or YouTube sometimes? Use them in Safari. Curbies adds pause cards, an on-screen clock and a daily limit per site.

iOS doesn't let any app change scrolling inside another app. So apps get the gate, and websites in Safari get the slow-down.

QUESTIONS

Does it work with the Instagram app, or only Safari?

Both, differently. The app gets a gate before it opens. Scrolling inside the app can't be changed by anyone but Instagram, so the slow-down (pause cards, a clock, feeds that end) works on the website in Safari.

Do I need an account?

No. Your stickers, lessons and progress save to your own iCloud and follow you to a new iPhone automatically.

What can Curbies see?

Not which apps you pick: Screen Time hands it anonymous tokens that stay on your phone. The Safari extension only runs on the social sites you allow, and nothing is sent to us.

What if I really need an app?

Open Curbies, take the pause and unlock it for the time you choose. If you've sealed a pact, an emergency exit is always there: type a phrase, wait 15 minutes, confirm.

Curbies is in testing for iPhone. Email us and we'll tell you when it's on the App Store.
