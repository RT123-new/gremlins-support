PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 16 October 2026. This policy covers the Curbies iPhone app, its Safari extension and this support website. The developer is Reginald Troka.

Curbies uses on-device storage, Apple services for purchases, and optional iCloud progress sync. It has no developer-operated account or billing server. We do not sell your data or include advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.

DATA ON YOUR DEVICE

  • Screen Time selections: the apps, categories and websites you choose are represented by Apple's opaque selection tokens. The app and its extensions use them locally to apply shields and usage limits.
  • Rules and activity: settings, intentions, pending rule changes, unlock counters, Safari activity counters and learning progress are stored on your device. They support the features and progress displays you choose to use.
  • Safari access: on websites you permit, the extension can inspect and modify page content to apply controls such as stopping points and feed limits. This processing happens on your device. Browsing history and page content are not sent to the developer.
  • Notifications: optional trial and rule-change reminders are scheduled on your device. Notification permission can be changed in iPhone Settings. The app does not collect an email address or send email reminders.

OPTIONAL SIGN IN WITH APPLE AND ICLOUD

You can use the app without signing in. If you choose Sign in with Apple, Curbies requests no name or email. Apple supplies an app-specific sign-in identifier, which is kept in the device Keychain. A value derived from that identifier separates your progress in iCloud storage.

While signed in, Curbies uses your iCloud key-value storage to sync stickers, game levels and wins, protected-day and streak records, and completed lessons. Apple stores and delivers that copy under your iCloud account; the developer has no service that reads it. Protection rules, selected apps, intentions and Safari activity counters are not included in this sync.

Sync depends on iCloud availability and may be delayed. Apple's handling of its services is described in the Apple privacy policy.

APP STORE PURCHASES

Apple handles purchases, payment information, subscription renewal and refunds. The app reads verified StoreKit purchase information, such as the product and expiry date, to provide the correct plan. It caches entitlement information on the device for use by the app and Safari extension.

An app-generated purchase identifier is stored in the device Keychain and can be sent to Apple with a purchase. The developer does not receive your payment card details or upload your purchase records to a separate billing server.

THIS WEBSITE AND SUPPORT

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no signup form, mailing list or analytics added by the developer. GitHub processes website requests and technical information under its privacy statement.

If you open a support issue, the developer and other visitors can see your GitHub username and anything you post. Share only the information needed to describe the problem. GitHub hosts and retains those issue records under its policies.

Support currently uses public GitHub issues. Do not post account credentials, receipts, personal contact details or private screenshots. For a privacy request, ask for a private contact route without posting the personal details of your request.

YOUR CHOICES AND DELETION

  • Sign out: You → Sign Out stops sync on that device and removes its stored Apple sign-in identifier. Local progress and existing iCloud progress remain.
  • Erase cloud copy: You → Erase Cloud Copy queues removal of the linked iCloud progress and signs you out. Local progress, rules and app selections remain. A small deletion record stays in iCloud and on the device so other devices pause uploads when they receive it; signing in again does not automatically resume uploads.
  • Resume sync: after an erase or account change, choose Merge This Phone’s Progress to upload retained progress, or Restore Cloud Only to replace local progress with the current account's saved copy when Apple delivers it.
  • Delete account and data: You → Delete Account and Data turns off app and Safari protection, clears this phone's profile, progress, counters, rules, selections and stored sign-in identifiers, and queues removal of the linked iCloud progress. Deletion barriers and pending deletion records remain to prevent stale data returning. Other devices must reconnect to receive the erase. Afterwards, revoke the app's Apple permission in Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → this app → Delete. Apple's instructions explain this final Apple-controlled step.
  • Manage permissions: use iPhone Settings to manage Screen Time access, Safari extension access, notifications and Sign in with Apple.
  • Manage purchases: cancel subscriptions through Apple. Erasing progress or deleting the app does not cancel them.

Keep the app installed and reconnect the linked iCloud account while deletion is queued. Removing the app alone does not delete iCloud progress, and Keychain items and backups may remain. Apple controls the retention of its purchase records, iCloud services and backups. Curbies does not promise immediate delivery of a queued iCloud deletion. Deleting account data does not cancel an Apple subscription.
