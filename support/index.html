SUPPORT

Need help or found a bug? Open a support issue on GitHub. Include your iOS version, app version, what you expected and what happened.

GitHub issues are public. Do not post your email, Apple Account details, receipts, browsing history or screenshots containing personal information. For a privacy question, ask for a private contact route without sharing the details publicly.

APP PROTECTION ISN'T WORKING

Open Gremlins and check the protection status on Home. Follow its setup or repair action, allow Screen Time access and select the apps or categories you want to protect. Then open a selected app to check that its shield appears.

When an unlock window ends, check that the app becomes shielded again. A setup preview is practice; it does not prove that a real app is protected. Native app gates and the Safari extension have separate setup and controls.

SET UP THE SAFARI EXTENSION

  1. On recent iOS versions, open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions. On iOS 17, open Settings → Safari → Extensions.
  2. Enable Gremlins and allow it on the websites you want to protect. You can also manage access from Safari's page menu.
  3. In Gremlins, open You → Setup Guide and check that Safari protection is on.
  4. Open a supported feed in Safari: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, Facebook or Reddit. Return to the setup guide to check whether the extension was observed.

Test scrolling and a useful task such as search or messaging. Website layouts and permissions vary; a successful check on one site does not verify all sites. Safari controls affect websites opened in Safari, not those services' native apps.

WHY IS A RULE CHANGE WAITING?

Protection can run with editable rules. If you seal the optional Pact, stricter changes apply immediately and looser changes wait. The presets use 6 hours for Casual, 24 hours for Normal and 72 hours for Hardcore; the Rules screen shows your selected cooling-off period.

When a request is ready, return to Rules and hold the confirmation control for 5 seconds. You have 24 hours to confirm before it expires. A replacement request of the same type starts a new wait.

TURN OFF PROTECTION OR USE EMERGENCY EXIT

Without a sealed Pact, you can turn off native app protection directly. With a Pact, the normal request uses your cooling-off period.

For the shorter route, open Rules → Emergency Exit, type the displayed phrase and request the exit. Wait 15 minutes, then return to Rules and hold to confirm. Confirmation turns native app protection off and unseals the Pact. Protection remains off until you enable it again.

Safari protection is separate. Its switch is in the setup guide and can be subject to a sealed Pact. You can also manage the extension's access in Safari settings.

CANCEL A TRIAL OR SUBSCRIPTION

Open iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Gremlins, then choose the cancellation option. Review the end date shown by Apple. If you do not want a free or discounted trial to renew, Apple advises cancelling at least 24 hours before it ends. Apple's cancellation instructions explain the current steps.

Deleting the app, signing out or erasing synced progress does not cancel a subscription. Purchases and renewal are handled by Apple. For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem service.

RESTORE A PURCHASE

Open Shop → Restore while using the Apple Account that made the purchase. An internet connection may be needed to refresh the App Store record. Optional Sign in with Apple for progress sync is separate from the Apple Account used for App Store purchases.

SIGN IN AND SYNC PROGRESS

Sign in with Apple is optional. In You, sign in to enable progress sync through your own iCloud. Gremlins requests neither your name nor your email.

Sync includes stickers, game progress, protected-day and streak records, and completed lessons. App selections, protection rules, browsing activity and intentions stay on the device. Use the same sign-in and iCloud accounts on your devices, and check the sync status in You. Apple manages delivery, so changes may take time to appear.

If iCloud is unavailable, progress stays on your phone. After an erase or account change, sync pauses. Choose Merge This Phone’s Progress to combine retained progress with the current account, or Restore Cloud Only to replace local progress with that account's saved copy when Apple delivers it. Unsynced local progress is lost if you choose Restore Cloud Only.

SIGN OUT OR ERASE SYNCED DATA

You → Sign Out stops sync on that device and removes its stored sign-in identifier. It keeps local progress and the iCloud copy.

You → Erase Cloud Copy queues deletion of the linked iCloud progress and signs you out. Other devices pause uploads when they receive the deletion marker. Local progress, rules and app selections remain. A small deletion record remains to stop older progress from being uploaded automatically.

Allow iCloud time to receive the change before removing the app. Removing the app alone does not erase the iCloud copy, and Keychain items or device backups may remain. See the privacy policy for the data involved.

DELETE ACCOUNT AND DATA

Open You → Delete Account and Data and review the confirmation. This turns off app and Safari protection and clears this phone's sign-in, stickers, lessons, counters, rules, intentions and app selections. It queues deletion of the linked iCloud progress and retains deletion records to prevent old data returning.

Keep the app installed and reconnect the linked iCloud account while Apple delivers the erase. Other devices must reconnect to receive it; delivery may be delayed.

To finish revoking Apple permission, open Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → this app → Delete. Follow Apple's sign-in removal guide if your Settings layout differs. The app also shows these steps after local deletion.

Deleting an account does not cancel a subscription. Use Apple's subscription management to manage renewal.
